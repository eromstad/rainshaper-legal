The game

Rainshaper is a puzzle game: raise and lower the stone columns of a floating island so that the rain from its spring waters every field and keeps every cottage dry. There is one Daily Island each day, the same for every player, plus an Archive of past islands, a larger Watershed island each week and Practice islands. By using Rainshaper, you agree to these terms.

The free game and Premium

Nothing sold in Rainshaper changes the Daily Island: it is the same puzzle for everyone.

Privacy Policy

The short version: Rainshaper has no user accounts and no server of its own. Your progress, streak, purchase and settings stay on your device. The one thing that isn't purely local: the free game shows ads through Google's Mobile Ads SDK, which can use an advertising identifier only if you agree to it. It's explained below, with how to decline.

Information we collect

None, on our own servers. Rainshaper has no server of its own and uses no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The third parties it works with are the advertising SDK and the App Store (or Google Play), each described below.

Advertising

Rainshaper's free version shows ads through Google's Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob): a banner on the menu screens (never over the island while you play), at most one full-screen ad a day after solving an island (never in your first few solves), and a short ad you can choose to watch for a hint. The hint ad is always optional.

  • Your consent comes first. Where the law requires it (such as in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), Rainshaper shows Google's consent form before any ads, and you can change your answer anytime in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, the app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before any tracking-based ad targeting, as Apple requires. You can allow or decline in the system prompt, and Rainshaper plays exactly the same either way.
  • Personalised only with your yes. Ads are personalised only if you agree in both places that apply to you. If you decline, ads are requested as non-personalised: they aren't targeted by your interests. Even then, the ad SDK may use your device's advertising identifier to limit how often an ad repeats, prevent fraud and measure ads. You can reset your advertising identifier or turn off tracking in your device's settings.

Google's use of any data collected through the SDK is governed by Google's own privacy policy and its disclosures for how it uses information from apps that use its services, not by Rainshaper's developer. Rainshaper Premium removes ads (and this SDK's ad activity) entirely; Premium players are never asked about ad consent or tracking.

Your data

  • Local storage only. Your progress on each island, best moves, streak, statistics, Premium and your settings (sound, music, graphics, motion and privacy choices) live in your device's local storage.
  • Deleting your data. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes everything on your device. There is no account to delete and no server-side copy, because none exists. Premium can be restored on a reinstall from your Apple or Google account.

Sharing a result

When you share an island, Rainshaper makes the result card (an image and a short text summary) entirely on your device and hands it to whatever app you pick in your device's own share sheet. Rainshaper never uploads it anywhere.

In-app purchases

Premium is bought through Apple's (or Google's) standard in-app purchase system. Rainshaper's developer never receives your payment details; that's handled directly between you and the store under its own privacy policy. Rainshaper only learns whether you have Premium, so it can unlock it on your device.

Ratings

Rainshaper may ask whether you'd like to rate it, using Apple's built-in rating prompt. Apple handles the prompt and any rating you leave; Rainshaper doesn't see your answer.

Children's privacy

Rainshaper does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app has no accounts, no chat, and no messaging between players; the only third-party data collection is the advertising SDK described above, which is limited by Apple's own platform rules for apps used by a general audience.
